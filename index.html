Coffee Guide

Classic Espresso-Based Drinks

Quick reference for popular Australian café drinks.

Espresso

Concentrated coffee brewed by forcing hot water through finely-ground beans. Basis for many café drinks.

Flat White

Double espresso with velvety steamed milk and a thin layer of microfoam — an Australian favourite.

Cappuccino

Espresso with equal parts steamed milk and foam, often dusted with cocoa.

Latte

Espresso with more steamed milk and a light foam — smooth and milky.

Long Black

Hot water topped with espresso — stronger than an Americano and popular in Australia.

Piccolo

Single ristretto topped with steamed milk in a small cup — rich and compact.

Australian Coffee Culture

Why coffee matters Down Under

Australia has a strong café culture centred on quality espresso, skilled baristas and neighbourhood cafes. The Flat White originated here and the local scene values freshness, technique and a focus on milk texture.

Regional roasting, independent cafes, and busy brunch scenes make coffee a social ritual as much as a morning pick-me-up.

Find a Barista Job

Tips for finding part-time barista work in Australia

Get prepared with the right qualifications and a friendly, service-first attitude.

  • RSA / RCG: Responsible Service of Alcohol (or RCG) where required for venues serving alcohol.
  • Food handler certificate: Short courses or on-site training for safe food handling.
  • Build experience: Volunteer, do barista shifts, or roster practice at local cafés.
  • Where to look: Seek, Indeed, Sidekicker, local café Facebook groups and community job boards.
  • CV tips: Highlight barista experience, peak-hour availability, and customer service skills.
